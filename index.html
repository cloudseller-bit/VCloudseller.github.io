          Questions

          Quick answers
          How do I order?

          Create an account, deposit money in the Wallet section, add a plan or an item to your cart and tap Place order. Then open your order chat. The shop will talk to you there and give you the item.

          Which games can you farm?

          Steal an Egg, Grow a Garden and other games. Ask us on WhatsApp if your game is not listed.

          What does cloud AFK farming mean?

          Your game keeps farming on our cloud for the time you choose, so it does not use your phone or PC.

          How do referrals work?

          Type your name in the Refer a friend section to get your link. When a friend opens it and orders, their order shows your name. The shop checks it and gives you both the reward.

          How do deposits work?

          In the Wallet section, send money to the bKash or Nagad number shown, then enter the amount and the transaction ID. The shop checks it and adds the money to your balance.

          What are VIP levels?

          Every completed order counts toward your VIP level. Higher levels give you an automatic discount on new orders. You can see your level in the Wallet section.

          How do promo codes work?

          When the shop shares a promo code, type it in your cart. You get the better of your promo code or your VIP discount.

          How do I talk to the shop about my order?

          After you place an order, open the Chats section. The shop will message you there.

          How do I leave a review?

          Use the Ratings and reviews section. Pick your stars and write a short review. The shop approves it before it shows on the site.

          How do I install the app?

          On Android, tap Install app at the top of the page or use your browser menu. On iPhone, tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
